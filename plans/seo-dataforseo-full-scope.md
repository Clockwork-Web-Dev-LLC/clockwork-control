## What DataForSEO cannot replace

- Pushing and syncing listings to directories (Semrush Listing Management). DataForSEO is read-only. Recommended: BrightLocal Citation Builder, about $10 per location a month ($120 a year for the five aggregators; official page, search excerpt only) plus a one-time $60 to $96 per location for manual submissions (third party): $50 a month at 5 locations, $120 at 12. If a client changes address or hours often and needs continuous sync: Moz Local Lite, about $16.58 per location a month billed yearly (third party): $83 at 5, $199 at 12.
- Replying to Google reviews, publishing Google Business Profile posts, and owner-side insights (calls, direction requests, views). Recommended: Google's own Business Profile API, $0 a month (no price page found; 'free' is from memory, unverified). Needs Google's approval of the project (application form; 0 queries a minute until approved) and each client adding the agency as a profile manager. Until then: by hand in Google's interface, $0.
- Review requests to customers (Semrush Local Pro). Recommended: a simple email with the profile's review link from the app's existing mail setup, $0; or drop. Moz Local Preferred includes it at about $24.92 per location a month (third party) if a vendor is preferred.
- Answering Google Q&A. Google shut the Q&A API on 2025-11-03 (third party, several sources agree). No route exists; drop, $0.
- Yelp and Facebook review monitoring. DataForSEO covers Google, Trustpilot and Tripadvisor only. Recommended: drop unless a client needs it; a review vendor such as Whitespark was quoted by the researcher at $79 per location a month (official page, search excerpt only, not re-checked by me).
- Perplexity, Copilot, Meta AI and Grok answers as real users see them. DataForSEO scrapes only ChatGPT and Gemini (plus Google AI Mode and AI Overviews). Recommended: Perplexity's developer answer through DataForSEO LLM Responses, labelled as an API answer, about $14 a month at an unverified $0.01 a call; or drop it, $0.
- Search Console's Generative AI impressions. No API anywhere. Stays a hand-entered number each month (already built), $0.
- Semrush's past data: daily rank history for the 1,500 keywords, Site Audit error trend, AI Visibility history, backlink audit decisions. DataForSEO cannot backfill these. Recommended: export each from Semrush before the cancel and import as labelled series, $0. DataForSEO can supply its own monthly ranking-count history back to October 2020 for about $2.49 once.
- Semrush's own scores: Keyword Difficulty %, Authority Score, Toxicity Score, Site Health, visibility % and share of voice. Recommended: DataForSEO's difficulty, rank, spam score and on-page score plus our own visibility maths, shown under their own names with a one-time methodology note, $0. Trend lines restart.
- Disavow file upload and link-building outreach email. Google has no disavow API and DataForSEO sends nothing. Recommended: the app exports the disavow text file and a person uploads it; Claude drafts outreach in Gmail, $0. Drop both if nobody uses them today.
- Duplicate-listing suppression and listing lock (a listing-network feature). Recommended: BrightLocal's duplicate removal add-on at 20% of the manual submission cost (as reported by the researcher, not re-checked), or drop.

## Effect on the cancel date

The 2027-01-08 date in the Exit criterion section (plan lines 1734 to 1769) no longer holds for the whole subscription. It rested on Phases 1 and 2 only, on seat holders confirming nothing else is used (criterion 6, now answered: everything is), and on nobody logging into Semrush for a full billing cycle from early November (criterion 5), which cannot be true while the team still relies on Position Tracking, Site Audit, Local and AI Visibility and still has exports to take. The new scope adds four large and two medium builds, each needing a parallel run (rank tracking a few weeks of overlap, Site Audit at least two crawls per site, Local a completed listing move and Google's approval). My estimate, not a researched fact: a full cancel is realistic at the 2027-04-08 renewal at the earliest and more likely 2027-05-08 or 2027-06-08; each month still costs about $1,028, less whatever has been dropped in stages. The exit criterion should be rewritten per feature: live in Control, history exported, one overlapping month, then that part of Semrush goes.

Cancelling in stages is possible and worth doing, because Semrush's help centre says the base plan can be downgraded directly and add-ons or extra limits can be removed by a form (not self-service, so allow a few days before the 8th). Suggested stages, with savings to be confirmed from the invoice: (1) extra seats as soon as fewer people need a login, possibly at the 2026-12-08 renewal, about $80 each by third-party price lists; (2) the AI Visibility add-on once prompt tracking has run a month, $99 per domain a month (Semrush help article); (3) the base plan, downgraded or cancelled, once rank tracking, the Labs research tools and Site Audit have each run a clean overlapping month; (4) Local last, only after every client's listings have been re-submitted through the new vendor and review replies work through Google, at $30 or $60 per location a month; check first whether Local is on an annual term, since Semrush's pricing page says billed annually. By my own arithmetic from third-party prices, base plus seats is about $490 and the add-ons about $538 of the $1,027.70, so the add-ons are worth targeting early. Once everything is cancelled the replacement costs about $83 to $555 a month (middle case about $266), a saving of about $473 to $945 a month against the plan's $1,009 to $1,026.

## Biggest risks

- No official DataForSEO page could be read directly from this machine; every price is from search excerpts, and several official pages still show pre-July prices and the old $100 commitments. One test call per endpoint and a look at the account billing page must come before any number is treated as final.

**What it cannot do, and what fills the gap**

- Ready-made visibility score, estimated traffic and average position: not returned by the API. Build ourselves; simple once SERPs are stored (visibility = sum of a click-through weight per position across the keyword set; estimated traffic = that weight times search volume from the planned keyword metrics cache). The numbers will not equal Semrush's because the click-through curve differs, so trend lines restart.
- Share of voice: not returned. Build ourselves; simple (same weighted sum per domain, divided by the total for all tracked domains). Needs search volume for every tracked keyword, which is a small extra Labs purchase.
- Competitor tracking on the same keywords: no separate product, but it comes free. Each stored SERP already contains every domain's position down to the depth bought, so competitor ranks are a query on stored rows. Only limit: competitors below the purchased depth (or below the page where stop_crawl_on_match stopped) are unknown, stored as null.
- Cannibalization report: not returned. Build ourselves; simple (flag a keyword where two or more of the client's URLs appear in one SERP, or where the ranking URL changes between checks). The keyword-map module already plans a 'two pages target one keyword' warning that this can feed.
- Tags, keyword groups, notes: build ourselves (a tag table; trivial). The API 'tag' field is only a pass-through label for matching results to our rows.
- Semrush history for the 1,500 keywords: DataForSEO cannot backfill daily ranks for an exact location and device. Fill: export Position Tracking history per project from Semrush before cancelling and import it as CSV; tracking in Control starts from day one otherwise. (DataForSEO Labs historical SERPs are monthly and country-level only; from memory, unverified.)
- Alerts, weekly email digests, client-facing shareable dashboards and PDF rank reports: build ourselves if wanted (medium), or leave to Claude plus the existing monthly-report skill and Client Reports section.
- Automatic competitor discovery and keyword suggestions inside the tracker: partly ours for free (most frequent domains in stored SERPs); suggestions come from the separate keyword-ideas feature.
- Local pack as a true map rank grid: the organic SERP only shows the 3-pack as seen from one point. Proper map rank belongs to the Local feature (Google Maps / Local Finder endpoints), not this one.
- Guaranteed same-moment daily snapshot and Semrush's own data-quality handling (retries, personalization smoothing): we own retries, failed-task handling and day-to-day noise; plan already suggests 4-week smoothing in reports.

**What it cannot do, and what fills the gap**

- Three severity levels (errors, warnings, notices) and Semrush's issue wording: DataForSEO gives raw true/false checks in two levels only. Build it ourselves: a rule table in the app that maps each check to a category, a severity and a plain-language fix.
- Error trend over time: DataForSEO keeps a crawl 30 days and has no history between crawls. Build it ourselves: store each crawl's per-check counts and compare runs (new, fixed, still open).
- Site Health score comparable to Semrush's: onpage_score is a different formula, so the number will jump at the switch. Either show onpage_score with a note, or compute our own from stored errors and warnings; do not chart the two on one line.
- Core Web Vitals thematic report: only available from the crawl at the browser-rendering price (34 times basic). Fill from what the app already has: PageSpeed Insights, CrUX real-user data and GTmetrix, for the top pages, at no vendor cost.
- Thematic reports (crawlability, HTTPS, internal linking, markup, international/hreflang, performance): not provided as reports. Compute from stored pages, links, non_indexable, redirect_chains and microdata rows. Depth of DataForSEO's hreflang and HTTPS/mixed-content checks: not found; confirm on a test crawl.
- Scheduled re-crawl and the finished-crawl email: DataForSEO has no scheduler or email. Build it ourselves with the app's weekly job and existing notifications.
- Marking an issue as ignored or fixed, and comparing two crawls in a UI: build it ourselves (small) or drop.
- Crawling staging sites behind a login, and JS-rendered crawling at no extra cost (Semrush includes JS rendering in Guru; from memory, unverified): DataForSEO charges 10 times for JavaScript; HTTP auth support not found.
